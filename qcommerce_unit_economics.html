Which SKUs actually make money on quick commerce?

Most brands can tell you revenue by platform. Very few can tell you profit by SKU by platform, once margin, ads, discounts, returns and deductions come out, or why the payout never matches what was invoiced. This is what that looks like when it is built properly.

Figures are synthetic, for a fictional personal-care brand — the models are the ones I run in-house

Every platform settles in its own file, on its own cycle. Deductions arrive netted, returns arrive weeks after the sale, and credit notes land 30 to 60 days late against an invoice from two cycles back.

₹7.9L of this month's gap reduces sales, not costs. Book revenue at invoice value and let those credits land when they land, and net sales are overstated by 9.3% this month and understated next month — so the P&L swings for reasons nobody can explain in the review.

Built properly, every rupee of the gap has a cause and an owner, and the month closes on provisioned numbers.
